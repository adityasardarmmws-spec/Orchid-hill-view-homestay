Welcome to Orchid Hill View Homestay

Hidden in the peaceful hills of Sittong II, Orchid Hill View Homestay is the perfect destination for travelers looking to relax and reconnect with nature.

Wake up to fresh mountain air, stunning sunrise views, comfortable rooms, delicious homemade meals and genuine Bengali hospitality.

Our location also offers easy access to Ahaldara View Point, Orange Orchards, Mahananda Wildlife Sanctuary and many other beautiful destinations.
